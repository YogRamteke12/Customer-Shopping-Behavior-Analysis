# Retail Customer Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** analyzing retail customer purchasing behavior using **SQL, Python, and Power BI**. The project transforms raw transaction data into actionable business insights related to customer spending, product performance, discounts, subscriptions, and customer segmentation.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze customer transaction data and identify meaningful patterns that can support business decision-making.

The analysis focuses on:

* Customer spending patterns
* Product popularity and review ratings
* Discount effectiveness
* Subscription behavior
* Customer segmentation
* Revenue contribution across customer demographics

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **3,900 customer transaction records** with **18 attributes**, including:

* Customer ID
* Age
* Gender
* Item Purchased
* Category
* Purchase Amount (USD)
* Location
* Size
* Color
* Season
* Review Rating
* Subscription Status
* Shipping Type
* Discount Applied
* Promo Code Used
* Previous Purchases
* Payment Method
* Frequency of Purchases

---

## 🔍 Business Questions

The SQL analysis addresses the following business questions:

1. What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?
2. Which customers used discounts but still spent above the average purchase amount?
3. What are the top 5 products based on average review rating?
4. How does average purchase amount vary by shipping type?
5. How does spending differ between subscribers and non-subscribers?
6. Which products have the highest discount usage rate?
7. How can customers be segmented into New, Returning, and Loyal customers?
8. What are the top 3 most purchased products within each category?
9. What is the subscription behavior of repeat buyers?
10. How does revenue contribution vary across different age groups?

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis highlights several important retail customer behavior patterns:

* Subscribers and non-subscribers demonstrate different spending behavior.
* Certain products achieve stronger customer review ratings.
* Discount usage is associated with customer purchasing behavior.
* Customer segmentation reveals distinct purchasing patterns across customer groups.
* Revenue contribution varies across different demographic groups.
